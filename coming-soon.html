SynthFlow

Learn flow arts with a 3D coach that throws, spins and hoops in front of you. SynthFlow is in testing and will open on the web, Android and iPhone soon.

What’s coming

  • A 3D coach

    Watch any movement from any angle, slow it down, and follow the throws, planes and hand paths step by step.

  • Practise on the beat

    Routines counted in bars with a spoken count-in, and music matched to the count: beat-measured tracks, generated percussion at any tempo, or your own songs.

  • Learn and unlock

    From the three-ball cascade and the waist hoop to five balls, Mills Mess and staff isolations. Master a movement and harder ones unlock.

  • Programs and progress

    Multi-week programs, custom routines, streaks, practice reminders, and learn pages on siteswap, timing, planes and safety.

  • Camera feedback

    Hear when your throws rush or drag the beat or your staff leaves its plane. Video never leaves your phone.

  • Optional extras

    Bluetooth heart rate, a Wear OS companion with a haptic beat, show your practice on a TV, and sync to your own Google Drive.
